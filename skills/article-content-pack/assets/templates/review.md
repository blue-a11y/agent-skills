# Review Checklist

## Content Accuracy

- [ ] Topic and target audience match `brief.yaml`
- [ ] Facts checked or marked as needing checking
- [ ] No unsupported numbers, screenshots, quotes, or examples
- [ ] No fake case, fake data, or fake endorsement

## Platform And Compliance

- [ ] No guaranteed outcomes or exaggerated promises
- [ ] Copyright and material authorization checked
- [ ] Personal information and privacy checked
- [ ] AI-assisted content labeling decision made
- [ ] Sensitive-topic risks checked when relevant

## Package Consistency

- [ ] `article.md` does not include a title
- [ ] `article.md` includes summary, body, and references when needed
- [ ] Summary is within 30 Chinese characters unless the user requested otherwise
- [ ] `topics.md` matches the article audience and platform
- [ ] `cover.txt` does not add stronger claims than the article supports
- [ ] `publish.md` includes at least 5 title options in different styles
- [ ] `publish.md` includes at least 5 description options, each no more than 30 Chinese characters
- [ ] `cover.txt` includes one cover prompt for each `publish.md` title option
- [ ] Cover count and content align one-to-one with the recommended title options
- [ ] At least 5 cover images exist under `covers/` as `.jpg`, `.jpeg`, or `.png`, or missing images are documented below
- [ ] Image generation used `gpt-image2`, or the model limitation is documented below
- [ ] `publish.md` title options, description options, caption, hashtags, and pinned comment match the article
- [ ] Recommended publishing times fit the target platform and audience, without promising traffic results
- [ ] CTA matches the content goal

## Decision

- [ ] Approve
- [ ] Revise
- [ ] Reject
